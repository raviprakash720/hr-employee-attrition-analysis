# HR Employee Attrition Analysis

## 📌 Project Objective

This project analyzes employee data to understand the reasons behind attrition (employees leaving the company).

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Google Colab

---

## 📊 Key Analysis Performed

* Data cleaning and preprocessing
* Exploratory Data Analysis 
* Employee satisfaction vs attrition
* Promotion and salary impact
* Workload and project distribution

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Employees with low satisfaction are more likely to leave
* Lack of promotion significantly increases attrition
* Employees with 2–3 years tenure tend to switch jobs
* Overworked employees (too many projects) show higher attrition

---

## ⚠️ Risk Analysis

A simple risk score was created to identify employees likely to leave based on:

* Satisfaction level
* Number of projects
* Promotion history
* Salary level

---

## 📌 Conclusion

The analysis shows that employee satisfaction, career growth, and workload balance are key factors affecting retention.
